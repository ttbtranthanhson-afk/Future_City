KỊCH BẢN CHI TIẾT
SCENE 1: A DREAM ABOUT THE FUTURE

Bối cảnh: Quảng Ninh năm 2026.
Trên sân khấu có một chiếc bàn, vài chiếc ghế và một tấm bản đồ Quảng Ninh. Minh và Lan đang trò chuyện về tương lai của quê hương mình.

Narrator: Welcome to Quang Ninh, a beautiful province in northern Vietnam. Today, in 2026, Quang Ninh is famous for Ha Long Bay, its coal mining industry, and its beautiful natural landscapes. However, what will this city look like in the next 50 years? Let's find out!

Minh: Hey, Lan! Have you ever wondered what Quang Ninh will look like in 2076?

Lan: Of course! I think it will become a much more modern city, with skyscrapers, flying cars and robots everywhere!

Minh: Wow! That sounds amazing! But I also hope our beautiful Ha Long Bay will still be there.

Lan: I agree. I don't want our city to become so modern that we lose our natural beauty.

Minh: You know what? I wish we could travel to the future and see it with our own eyes!

(Đột nhiên có tiếng nhạc khoa học viễn tưởng. Alex xuất hiện cùng Robo.)

Alex: Hello, everyone! Did someone say they wanted to visit the future?

Lan: Who are you? And why are you dressed like that?

Alex: I'm Alex, a tour guide from the year 2076. And this is my assistant, Robo!

Robo: Hello, humans! I am Robo, an artificial intelligence robot. My intelligence level is 99.99 percent!

Minh: Seriously? Are you really from the future?

Alex: Absolutely! Would you like to see what Quang Ninh looks like in 50 years?

Lan: Yes! We can't wait!

Alex: Then, everyone, get ready! We are going to the future in 3... 2... 1!

(Nhạc du hành thời gian vang lên. Các diễn viên di chuyển và thay đổi đạo cụ. Ánh sáng sân khấu chuyển sang màu xanh hoặc tím)
SCENE 2: WELCOME TO QUANG NINH 2076!
Bối cảnh: Quảng Ninh năm 2076 – một thành phố thông minh, hiện đại và xanh.

(Alex và Robo đưa Minh, Lan đến thành phố tương lai.)

Narrator: Welcome to Quang Ninh in 2076! After 50 years of development, the city has become one of the most modern and environmentally friendly cities in Vietnam.
Technology, tourism, healthcare and education have all made incredible progress.

PART 1: A SMART AND MODERN CITY
Minh: Oh my goodness! Look at those buildings! They are so tall and beautiful!

Lan: And look! There are flying cars in the sky! I can't believe what I'm seeing!

Alex: Welcome to our smart city! Almost everything here is powered by clean energy, such as solar and wind power.

Engineer: That's right! As an engineer, I can tell you that technology has completely changed our daily lives. Our city now has smart transportation systems, self-driving buses and underground trains. People can travel quickly without causing traffic jams.

Robo: Beep! Beep! I can also help people with their daily activities, deliver packages and answer questions.

Minh: That's fantastic! Does that mean people don't have to work anymore?

Robo: Not exactly! Humans still need to work. I just help them save time and do their jobs more efficiently!

(Mọi người cười.)

PART 2: A GREEN AND CLEAN ENVIRONMENT
Lan: But what about the environment? Has Ha Long Bay changed a lot?

Environmental Scientist: I'm glad you asked! Protecting the environment has always been one of our top priorities. In 2076, the water in Ha Long Bay is much cleaner. We use advanced technology to treat wastewater and reduce plastic pollution. We also have more green spaces, public parks and forests.

Minh: That's wonderful! I was afraid that industrial development would destroy our natural landscapes.

Fisherman: Don't worry! I'm a local fisherman, and my life has changed a lot. In the past, fishing was difficult because of water pollution and unpredictable weather. Now, we use modern equipment to monitor the sea and protect marine life. There are also strict rules to prevent overfishing.

Lan: So, people can still enjoy fishing while protecting nature?

Fisherman: Exactly! We have learned that nature is not just a resource. It is something we must protect for future generations.

PART 3: TOURISM AND CULTURAL DEVELOPMENT
Minh: What about tourism? Ha Long Bay is already famous, but I wonder how it will develop in the future.

Tourism Manager: Tourism has become one of the most important industries in Quang Ninh.
Visitors from all over the world come here to explore Ha Long Bay, Bai Tu Long Bay, Yen Tu Mountain and many other attractions.
We also have underwater museums, virtual reality experiences and eco-friendly hotels.

Lan: Underwater museums? That sounds incredible!

Tourism Manager: Yes! Tourists can explore the beauty of the ocean through special glass tunnels without harming marine life. However, we don't only focus on modern attractions. We also preserve traditional festivals, local food and historical sites.

Minh: That's great! I think preserving our culture is just as important as developing the economy.

Tourism Manager: Absolutely! We want visitors to experience both the modern lifestyle and the traditional beauty of Quang Ninh.

PART 4: A BETTER LIFE FOR EVERYONE
Lan: I have another question. Has people's quality of life improved?

Doctor: Of course! I'm a doctor at Quang Ninh International Smart Hospital. Nowadays, hospitals use artificial intelligence and advanced medical equipment to detect diseases earlier and provide better treatment. People can also have online medical consultations, which saves them a lot of time.

Minh: That's amazing! What about education?

Robo: I can answer that question! Schools in 2076 are equipped with smart classrooms, virtual reality technology and AI learning assistants. Students can learn about history by visiting virtual historical sites and explore science through realistic simulations.

Lan: So, students don't need to carry heavy school bags anymore?

Robo: Correct! Most learning materials are digital. Your school bag will finally get a holiday!

(Mọi người cười.)

PART 5: NEVER FORGET THE PAST
(Một người thợ mỏ lớn tuổi bước ra sân khấu.)

Elderly Miner: Excuse me, young people. You have talked a lot about technology and modern development. But do you know what made Quang Ninh special in the past?

Minh: Was it the coal mining industry?

Elderly Miner: Exactly! For many generations, coal mining played an important role in the development of Quang Ninh. Many workers spent their lives working hard in the mines to support their families and contribute to the country. Today, the mining industry has become much safer and more environmentally friendly thanks to modern technology.

Lan: That's really meaningful. We should never forget the people who helped build our hometown.

(Thị trưởng xuất hiện.)

Future Mayor: Well said! As the Mayor of Quang Ninh, I believe that our development is not only about economic growth. Our greatest achievement is creating a city where people can live happily, work safely and enjoy a clean environment. We have invested in technology, education, healthcare and public transportation. But most importantly, we have never forgotten our history, culture and traditions.

Minh: Your city is truly amazing! But I have one final question. What is the biggest challenge Quang Ninh has faced during these 50 years?

Future Mayor: The biggest challenge was finding a balance between economic development and environmental protection. At first, it wasn't easy. But through cooperation, innovation and determination, we made it possible.

Environmental Scientist: We learned that a city cannot be truly successful if its environment is damaged.

Engineer: Technology should make people's lives better, not create more problems.

Doctor: Everyone deserves access to good healthcare.

Tourism Manager: Tourism should bring economic benefits while respecting nature and local culture.

Fisherman: And we must protect the sea for future generations.

Elderly Miner: We should always remember the hard work of previous generations.

Robo: A perfect city needs both intelligent technology and intelligent humans!

(Mọi người cười.)

ENDING: THE FUTURE STARTS TODAY
Lan: I can't believe what we've just experienced. Quang Ninh in 2076 is even more wonderful than I imagined!

Minh: Yes! But I have realized something important. The future doesn't just happen by itself. It depends on what we do today.

Alex: Exactly! Every small action today can create a better tomorrow.

Future Mayor: The future of Quang Ninh belongs to the younger generation. You are the people who will turn today's dreams into tomorrow's reality.

Narrator: And now, it is time for Minh and Lan to return to 2026. They have learned that building a better city requires everyone's efforts.

(Nhạc du hành thời gian vang lên. Minh và Lan quay lại vị trí ban đầu.)

Lan: We're back! Was that all just a dream?

Minh: Maybe. But I know one thing for sure: I want to contribute to making Quang Ninh a better place.

Lan: Me too! Let's start with small actions, such as protecting the environment, studying hard and preserving our local culture.
